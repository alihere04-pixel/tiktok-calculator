## The Profit Formula: Revenue − All Costs = Real Profit

The formula is simple. Getting every variable right is the hard part.

```
Real Profit = Selling Price
  − Seller Discount
  − Platform Discount
  − Category Commission Fee
  − Transaction Fee
  − Fixed/Per-Order Fees
  − Affiliate/Creator Commission
  − Refund Administration Fee (US only)
  − Cost of Goods (COGS)
  − Outbound Shipping Cost
  − Ad Spend per Unit (CPA)
  = Net Profit Per Unit
```

Every line above is a real cost. Skip any of them, and your margin is fiction.

## Every Fee You Must Account For (7 Categories)

| Fee Type | Who Pays | When It Applies | Typical Range |
|----------|----------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Category Commission** | Seller | Every sale | 3–18% depending on market & category |
| **Transaction Fee** | Seller | Every sale | ~1.5–3% + fixed amount |
| **Fixed/Per-Order Fees** | Seller | Every order | $0.10–$1.50 per order |
| **Affiliate/Creator Commission** | Seller | When enabled | 5–20% of net selling price |
| **Refund Admin Fee** | Seller | US refunds only | 20% of referral fee, capped at $5 |
| **Seller Discount** | Seller | When you run a sale | Your choice (reduces commission base) |
| **Platform Discount** | TikTok | Promotional vouchers | TikTok-funded (reduces their cut only) |

**Key insight:** Commission is calculated on the **net selling price** (after seller discount, before platform discount). Platform discounts lower TikTok's cut but not your cost basis—so they actually *improve* your margin.

## Hidden Costs Most Sellers Miss

### 1. Customer Shipping ≠ Your Shipping Cost
In **US**: Customer shipping is *excluded* from commission base.  
In **UK, SG, MY, PH**: Customer shipping is *included* in commission base.  
This means a £5 shipping charge in the UK adds £0.45 (9%) to your commission bill.

### 2. Affiliate Commission Compounds
If you enable Open Collaboration at 15% and a creator sells your $50 product:
- Commission base = $50 (or net after seller discount)
- Affiliate fee = $7.50
- *Then* TikTok takes their 6% commission on the *same* $50
- You pay both. They stack.

### 3. Refund Administration Fee (US Only)
US sellers pay a refund admin fee: **20% of the referral fee, capped at $5 per SKU**.  
At a 5% return rate on a $50 item with 6% commission: that's $0.30 per unit sold—whether it's returned or not.

### 4. New Seller Promo Expiry
Promotional rates (e.g., 1.8% for 90 days) expire. Your margin *will* drop when they do. Model both promo and standard rates.

## Common Mistakes That Kill Margins

| Mistake | Impact | Fix |
|---------|--------|-----|
| Using headline commission only | Misses tiered rates, promos, exceptions | Check your exact category on [market fee page](/us/tiktok-shop-fees) |
| Ignoring transaction fees | Loses 1.5–3% + fixed per sale | Always include in calculator |
| Forgetting affiliate fees | 5–20% surprise deduction | Model affiliate *before* launching campaigns |
| Not modeling promo expiry | Margin drops 1–3 pp unexpectedly | Calculate both promo & standard rates |
| Treating customer shipping as free money | In 4/5 markets, it raises commission base | Enter customer shipping in calculator |
| Using markup instead of margin | $50 cost + 50% markup = $75 price → 33% margin | Target margin, back-calculate price |
