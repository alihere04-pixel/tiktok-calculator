## The Profit Formula: Revenue − All Costs = Real Profit

The formula is simple. Getting every variable right is the hard part.

```
Real Profit = Selling Price
  − Platform Fees (commission, transaction, fixed, affiliate)
  − Cost of Goods (COGS)
  − Outbound Shipping Cost
  − Ad Spend per Unit (CPA)
  = Net Profit Per Unit
```

Every line above is a real cost. Skip any of them, and your margin is fiction.

Two things that surprise people, and that we model the way TikTok actually bills:

- **Money a buyer pays for shipping is not your revenue.** You collect it and pass it to the carrier. Our calculator counts it as a cost on the commission base (in some markets) but never as income.
- **A platform-funded discount is not your revenue either.** It reduces what the buyer pays, and TikTok funds the difference.

**Key insight:** the commission base is **not** the same in every market, and this is where manual estimates go wrong:

- **US, UK** — base = (selling price − your discount) **+ customer shipping + platform discount**
- **SG** — base = (selling price − your discount) **+ customer shipping**
- **MY, PH** — base = selling price − your discount. Customer shipping is *excluded*.

So a US or UK seller who charges a buyer $5 for shipping is charged commission on $5 more than a Malaysian or Philippine seller would be for the identical charge.

## How Category Commission Rates Work

Commission isn't one number. It varies by:

1. **Market** — US, UK, Malaysia, Singapore, Philippines each have different rate cards
2. **Category** — in the US, 185 of 202 categories sit at 6% and 17 sit at 5% (Jewelry and Pre-Owned). In the UK, 217 categories pay 9% and 129 pay 5% (Phones & Electronics, Computers & Office, most Beauty, Pre-Owned)
3. **Seller Tier** — Malaysia and Singapore have BXP / non-BXP and programme tiers with different rates; the Philippines has Marketplace vs Mall
4. **Sales thresholds** — 16 US categories (Collectibles and Pre-Owned) charge the normal rate on your first $10,000 in sales, then only 3% on the portion above it

## Hidden Costs Most Sellers Miss

### 1. Customer Shipping Changes Your Commission Base
In **US, UK, SG**: customer shipping is **included** in the commission base.  
In **MY, PH**: customer shipping is **excluded**.  
A $5 shipping charge in the UK therefore adds commission on that $5. The same charge in the Philippines does not.

### 2. Affiliate Commission Compounds
Affiliate commission is charged on **selling price minus your discount** — in every market. On top of that, TikTok's own commission still applies, and in US/UK/SG that commission base also includes customer shipping.

If you enable Open Collaboration at 15% on a $50 product:
- Affiliate fee = 15% × ($50 − your discount)
- TikTok commission = its rate × (($50 − your discount) + customer shipping [+ platform discount in US/UK])

You pay both. They stack. The 15% above is an example rate you choose in Seller Center, not a published TikTok rate — our calculator lets you enter 0–80%.

### 3. Refund Administration Fee (US Only)
US sellers pay a refund admin fee: **20% of the referral fee, capped at $5 per SKU** (effective 15 May 2025).  
At a 5% return rate on a $52.50 commission base: that's about $0.03 per unit sold — charged whether or not you keep the sale.

### 4. New Seller Promotions Are Not Published Rates
Be careful here, because this is widely misquoted:

- **US** — our rate data carries a promotional rate of **1.8%**, but TikTok does not publish it, so we label it *needs verification* internally. Model it, but do not plan around it.
- **UK** — a new seller promotion exists within **45 days of onboarding**, conditional on completing the required **Seller Missions**. The exact rate is not published. Our data holds a 0% row at medium confidence, and our calculator **deliberately does not charge 0%** — pricing a fee at zero would overstate your profit, so it shows the standard rate and flags the promotion as unpriced.
- **MY, SG, PH** — no new seller promotion appears in our current rate data.

When a promo ends, your margin drops. Model the standard rate as your real cost and treat the promo as upside.

## Example Calculation: $50 Product (US Market, 6% Category)

| Component | Amount | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Selling Price | $50.00 | Listed price |
| Seller Discount | −$5.00 | 10% off sale |
| Platform Discount | −$3.00 | TikTok-funded voucher |
| Customer Shipping charged to buyer | $4.50 | Pass-through, not your revenue |
| **Commission Base** | **$52.50** | ($50 − $5) + $4.50 + $3.00 |
| Referral Fee (6%) | −$3.15 | Charged on the $52.50 base |
| Transaction Fee | $0.00 | **The US has no separate transaction fee** |
| Fixed / Platform Support Fee | $0.00 | **The US has no per-order platform fee** |
| Affiliate (None) | $0.00 | |
| Refund Admin Fee (5% return rate) | −$0.03 | 5% × (20% × $3.15) |
| **Total Platform Fees** | **−$3.18** | |
| COGS | −$18.00 | Landed cost |
| Outbound Shipping | −$4.50 | Your cost to ship |
| Ad Spend (CPA) | −$3.00 | Blended cost per sale |
| **Net Profit** | **$21.32** | |
| **Profit Margin** | **42.6%** | Profit ÷ Selling Price |

Two things this table shows that a mental estimate never would:

1. **Your effective take rate (6.36%) is higher than the headline 6%**, because customer shipping and the platform discount both sit inside the commission base.
2. **A naive estimate overstates profit by $6.18.** Subtracting only COGS and shipping gives $27.50. Real profit is $21.32 — a **22% overestimation**.

## Common Mistakes That Kill Margins

| Mistake | Impact | Fix |
|---------|--------|-----|
| Using headline commission only | Misses tiered rates, thresholds, exceptions | Check your exact category on [market fee page](/us/tiktok-shop-fees) |
| Ignoring transaction fees | Up to 3.78% extra in MY, SG, PH | Always include in calculator |
| Assuming a fixed fee exists everywhere | Only MY (RM 0.54) and PH (PHP 5) have one | Check per market — US and UK have none |
| Forgetting affiliate fees | Doubles your fee load when creators are on | Model affiliate *before* launching campaigns |
| Treating a promo rate as permanent | Margin jumps the day it ends | Calculate the standard rate as your baseline |
| Getting the shipping base backwards | Wrong commission in every market | US/UK/SG include it, MY/PH do not |
| Using markup instead of margin | $50 cost + 50% markup = $75 price → 33% margin | Target margin, back-calculate price |
