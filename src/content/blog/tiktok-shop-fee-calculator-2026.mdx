Every TikTok Shop seller asks the same question before listing a product: **after TikTok takes its cut, what do I actually keep?**

The honest answer is "it depends" — and it depends on more than most sellers expect. Commission changes by market, by category, and sometimes by which programme your shop is enrolled in. On top of commission, some markets add a transaction fee, some add a flat per-order fee, and a few add a pre-order fee you only pay if you opt in.

This guide gives you the 2026 numbers for all five markets we track, and the free tools to apply them to your own product.

## 2. Commission Rates by Market

Commission is the big one, but it is never a single number. Here is the real spread across the categories we track for each market.

| Market | Categories tracked | Commission range | Structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **US** | 202 | 5.00% – 6.00% | Flat rate, no seller tiers |
| **UK** | 347 | 5.00% – 9.00% | Flat rate, VAT inclusive |
| **Malaysia** | 6 category groups | 7.02% – 17.82% | BXP / non-BXP × Marketplace / Mall |
| **Singapore** | 5 clusters × 4 programmes | 4.36% – 8.175% | standard / BXP / BXP-restricted / BXP-mixed |
| **Philippines** | 63 | 5.00% – 7.30% Marketplace, 5.00% – 8.70% Mall | Marketplace / Mall |

Sources: [US Seller Center](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5988482086864682), [UK Seller Center](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3315312175236897), [MY Seller Center](https://seller-my.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6907739532281602), [SG Seller Center](https://seller-sg.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2161524467910401), [PH Seller Center](https://seller-ph.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3157977859229442).

### United States — simple, with one exception

185 of 202 US categories sit at **6.00%**. The 17 at **5.00%** are all Jewelry (Diamond, Gold, Jade, Platinum, Ruby/Sapphire & Emerald) and Pre-Owned goods. There are no seller tiers in the US — your rate depends only on the category.

### United Kingdom — a 5% tier and a 9% default

129 UK categories pay **5.00%**: Phones & Electronics, Computers & Office Equipment, most of Beauty & Personal Care, and Pre-Owned items. The remaining 217 pay **9.00%**, which is the default for anything unlisted. UK rates are **VAT inclusive**, so 9% covers the commission and the VAT together.

### Malaysia — four rates per category

Malaysia is the most tiered market we track. A category's rate depends on whether your shop is **BXP** (Brand Exchange Partner) or not, and whether the listing sits in the **Marketplace** or the **Mall**.

| Category | BXP Marketplace | BXP Mall | non-BXP Marketplace | non-BXP Mall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electronics | 7.020% | 10.260% | 11.340% | 14.590% |
| Toys | *not published* | *not published* | 14.580% | 17.820% |

Published bands for the whole market: BXP Marketplace **4.86% – 9.18%**, BXP Mall **8.91% – 12.42%**, non-BXP Marketplace **11.34% – 17.82%**, non-BXP Mall **14.58% – 18.90%**.

### Singapore — four programmes

Singapore splits into *standard*, *BXP*, *BXP-restricted* and *BXP-mixed*:

| Cluster | standard | BXP | BXP restricted | BXP mixed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unlisted / all other | 8.175% | 7.085% | — | — |
| Electronics & selected lifestyle | 7.085% | 5.450% | 7.085% | 4.360% |
| Other categories | 8.175% | 7.085% | 7.085% | 5.995% |

### Philippines — Marketplace vs Mall

Marketplace rates run **5.00% – 7.30%**; Mall rates run **5.00% – 8.70%**. If your category is not listed, the fallback is **6.70% Marketplace / 7.70% Mall**.

### What counts as the commission base

This is where most manual estimates go wrong, and the markets genuinely differ:

- **US, UK, SG** — customer shipping charged to the buyer is **added to the commission base**. In the US and UK, the platform-funded discount is added too.
- **Malaysia, PH** — customer shipping is **not** in the commission base. Commission is simply selling price minus your discount.

So a US seller charging a buyer $5 shipping is charged commission on $5 more than a Malaysian seller would be for the same charge.

## 3. Transaction Fees: What TikTok Takes Per Sale

A transaction fee is a second, separate charge on top of commission — and it only exists in three of our five markets.

| Market | Transaction fee | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **US** | **None** | Folded into the referral fee; there is no separate line |
| **UK** | **None** | No separate transaction fee in our UK data |
| **Malaysia** | **3.78%** | Tax inclusive, high confidence |
| **Singapore** | **3.27%** | Medium confidence |
| **Philippines** | **2.24%** | VAT inclusive |

Two details worth knowing. In Malaysia and the Philippines the transaction fee is calculated on a base that **includes** customer shipping, so shipping quietly inflates it. In Singapore the transaction fee base additionally includes the platform-funded discount.

If you are in the US or UK, do not go looking for this fee in your Seller Center statement — you will not find it, because the commission rate already absorbs it.

## 4. Fixed & Per-Order Fees by Market

Fixed fees do not scale with order value, which makes them brutal on cheap products and irrelevant on expensive ones.

| Market | Fixed / per-order fee | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| **Malaysia** | Platform Support Fee | **RM 0.54** per order (from 15 Feb 2026) |
| **Philippines** | Shipping Service Fee | **5.25% + PHP 5** per order (opt-in) |
| **US / UK / Singapore** | None | — |

Two more charges that are easy to miss because they only appear if you enrol:

- **Pre-order fee** — Malaysia **2.00%**, Singapore **1.09%**, Philippines **2.00%**. The US and UK have none in our data.
- **Singapore BXP service fee** — an extra **3.27%** on mixed BXP orders containing non-restricted items.

> **Watch your AOV.** At a $6 item, RM 0.54 per order is roughly 9% of revenue before commission. The fixed fee matters more than the rate difference between two categories.

### What that adds up to

Here is the platform take on a single order, before your own costs — one representative band per market, no seller discount and no affiliate.

| Market | Commission | Transaction | Fixed / optional | Platform take |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **US** | 6.00% | none | none | **6.00%** |
| **UK** (unlisted) | 9.00% | none | none | **9.00%** |
| **MY** (non-BXP Marketplace) | 11.34% | 3.78% | + RM 0.54/order | **15.12% + fixed** |
| **SG** (standard) | 8.175% | 3.27% | none | **11.445%** |
| **PH** (Marketplace fallback) | 6.70% | 2.24% | +5.25% + PHP 5 if opted in | **8.94% + optional** |

Two conclusions jump out. The **spread between markets is larger than the spread between categories within a market** — a US seller at 6% has a structural advantage over a Malaysian marketplace seller at 11.34% plus a 3.78% transaction fee. And in **Malaysia, Singapore and the Philippines, the transaction fee alone can exceed the entire US commission rate.**

## 5. New Seller Promotions & How to Qualify

Promotional commission rates are the most misread number in this whole space, because they are temporary and often not published.

**United States** — our rate data carries a promotional rate of **1.8%**, flagged internally as *needs verification* because TikTok does not publish it on Seller Center. Model it, but do not build your business plan on it.

**United Kingdom** — a new seller promotion exists within **45 days of onboarding**, conditional on completing the required **Seller Missions**. The exact rate is not published; our data records a 0% row at medium confidence, and our calculator deliberately **refuses to charge 0%** — pricing a fee at zero would flatter your margin, so we mark it *unpriced* instead and show the standard rate.

**Malaysia, Singapore, Philippines** — no new seller promotion appears in our current rate data.

**The practical rule:** always model the standard rate as your real cost. Run the promo as an upside scenario, and re-check your margin the day it expires.

## 6. Category Exceptions & Special Rules

**US — the $10,000 threshold.** 16 US categories have a tiered threshold: the normal rate applies to your first **$10,000** in sales, and only **3%** applies to the portion above it. Collectibles (6% base) and Pre-Owned goods (5% base) are the affected groups. If you cross the threshold, your effective rate drops sharply.

**Malaysia — missing tiers are not zero.** Toys has no published BXP rows. An unpublished (category, tier) pair is treated as *unpriced*, never as 0%.

**Singapore — restricted BXP items.** Virtual products, infant formula, gold decorations and semi-finished gold are charged at the standard BXP rate of **7.085%** with **no** BXP service fee, no matter how good your BXP terms look elsewhere. Mixed BXP orders, by contrast, combine a lower commission rate *and* the extra 3.27% service fee.

**Philippines — 9 categories are tier-agnostic.** Pre-Owned items and several Electronics rows (phones, laptops, audio/video, gaming consoles) carry the same rate whether you sell in Marketplace or Mall.

**US only — refund administration fee.** 20% of the referral fee, capped at **$5 per SKU**. Because it accrues on refunds, it is charged against you whether or not you keep the sale.
